What we mean by sustainability

The ability of a company to keep operating in a world that prices carbon, enforces labour and safety law, and holds boards accountable. We measure it on three pillars and always relative to sector peers.

Scoring

  1. Each metric → percentile rank within GICS sector (0–100). Reverse for "bad" metrics.
  2. Missing value → excluded from the pillar mean and a fixed penalty is subtracted. We never impute the median.
  3. Pillar score = mean of its metric ranks. Composite = user-weighted mean of pillars.

Limitations (honest list)

  • All metric values on this page are synthetic. The pipeline, schema and scoring are real; the numbers are seeded random draws calibrated per sector.
  • Sector-relative ranking hides that a "best-in-class" utility still emits far more than a bank.
  • Scope 3 is where most emissions sit for financials and retailers, and it is the least reported.
  • The fund model ignores pass-through of carbon costs to customers, and uses active share as a stand-in for tracking error.
