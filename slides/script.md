## The eight second version

The line for the corridor, the lift, the judge who arrives late:

> **We rebuilt the sustainability ranking ten thousand defensible ways. The
> median company's rank is an interval 312 places wide out of 500. So we stopped
> ranking and priced the risk instead.**

31 words, 14 seconds. Backup, for a listener who is not technical: *"Four in
five S&P 500 companies with a checkable climate promise are missing it, and they
are delivering less than a third of what they promised."*

## Beat 4. A Paris aligned fund sells the decarbonisers

**[1:38 to 2:02]  24 s  51 words**  Slide 4.

> The official answer is the EU Paris-Aligned Benchmark. We built it, article by
> article. 97.4 percent of its carbon cut is reallocation, not companies
> cutting. [Article 6 invites you to overweight nineteen companies cutting seven
> percent a year. Article 12 bans nine of them.] It is a screen, not a strategy.

Stage: the bracketed pair is the cut line if you are behind the clock. Losing it
costs 8 seconds and the slide's mono line still carries it in print.

**Do not say** that the cross term is positive because a PAB sells the fastest
cutters. It is true of the decomposition and it is the one sentence on this
slide a judge can turn around: the compliant book's held names cut at 5.7
percent a year against the index's 5.0. Article 6 against Article 12 is the same
claim with no counter-example in it.

## What changed from the first draft, and why

1. **Timed at 130 wpm, not 150.** The draft ran 165.5 seconds at 130, not the
   150 it claimed at 150 wpm. Cut 32 spoken words to land at 150.9.
2. **"Because the allocation runs on rank" added to beat 3.** The zero weight
   change is an identity, not a discovery. Saying so first is the difference
   between rigorous and naive.
3. **Beat 4 no longer claims a PAB sells the decarbonisers from the cross term.**
   `portfolio.json` `article7.organic` shows the compliant book's held names
   cutting at 5.71 percent a year against the index at 5.03. Article 6 versus
   Article 12, 19 qualifiers and 9 banned, says the same thing and cannot be
   turned around.
4. **Beat 5 uses 139 and 361, not 181.** 181 is the allocation lane's count, no
   Scope 1 from any source. 361 is the score lane's, no mandatory tonnage, and
   it is the split the "being measurable is a penalty" line is computed on.
   Mixing the two in consecutive sentences was a trap. 181 stays in the demo
   caption, where it is the right number.
5. **Demo caption reworded.** `$123m of the $1bn moves` overstated it: $123.3m
   is the swing in what the 181 non-filers are allocated between the four
   treatments, from $121.1m to $244.4m. The new caption says that.

Four Q&A answers in the first draft did not reproduce against the repo and are
corrected in `qa.md`: the 22 percent understatement figure, the +4.35 zero-fill
size loading, the 84-of-122 independent matcher agreement, and the
token_set_ratio 80 fuzzy-matching demonstration. Details in the fact-check
section of `qa.md`.
